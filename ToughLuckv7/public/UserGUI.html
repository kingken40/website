Welcome to Tough Luck!

Get ready to test your luck in this exciting game!

How to Play:

  • Choose the number of moves you want to play.
  • Select the Game Mode: TL-1, TL-2, or Endless.
  • Decide if you want to allow Duplicate Numbers in your path.
  • Enter your moves (integer from 0-9) based on the numbers generated by the game. (You'll be lucky to get one!)
  • Score points for correct moves and try to avoid wrong ones!
