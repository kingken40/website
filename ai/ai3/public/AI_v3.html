🎭 Personalities
  • Assistant — Clear, concise, and helpful by default. Great for quick answers, summaries, and action steps when you want straightforward guidance.
  • Creative — Generates fresh ideas and variations. Use for brainstorming names, activities, stories, or alternative approaches when you’re stuck.
  • Coder — Code‑first replies with runnable snippets and comments. Call this when you want examples, best practices, and notes on edge cases.
  • Teacher — Breaks concepts down step‑by‑step with analogies. Ideal for learning new topics or teaching others with clear checkpoints.
  • Funny — Light, friendly tone with mild humor or emojis. Good for casual chats; switch off for formal docs or professional emails.
  • Scholar — Thorough, structured, and precise. Helpful for deeper dives, comparisons, and careful reasoning with caveats.
