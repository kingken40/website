🤖 AI Models & Categories Guide

💡 What Model Categories Mean

N.O.V.A categorizes models into dedicated performance groups to help you choose the right engine for your workload:

🧠 Best for reasoning Advanced logic engines designed for deep problem solving, multi-step math, complex code debugging, algorithm design, and strategic analysis. These models think step-by-step before producing high-accuracy output.
⚡ Best for speed Low-latency, ultra-fast models optimized for instant answers, quick lookups, simple text formatting, and high-speed chat responses where speed is priority #1.
🎨 Best for creativity Models tuned for creative writing, storytelling, natural conversation, tone matching, and open-ended brainstorming.
📚 For long context Models with massive context windows built to read, summarize, and analyze huge documents, codebases, or long conversation histories without losing track of details.
⚡ Recently Used Automatically stores your up to 7 most recently active models (most recent on top, ordered from top-to-bottom by usage) for fast one-click switching.
